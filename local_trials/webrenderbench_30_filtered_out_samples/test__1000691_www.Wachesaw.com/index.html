Wachesaw Plantation Club

Wachesaw Plantation Club was founded in 1985 as the premier private Country Club on South Carolina’s Hammock Coast. Located in the quaint seaside town of Murrells Inlet, we are far from the hustle and bustle but within easy reach of all area conveniences and attractions.  The Club is located in the historic Wachesaw Plantation gated community. 

Our Mission is to provide Members and their families an exclusive, friendly and inviting active lifestyle. World Class Golf, Tennis, Recreational Activities, and Social Activities provide the centerpieces for Members to connect, make friends, and experience life to the fullest.  Our facilities include an 18-hole Tom Fazio designed golf course, 8 outdoor clay tennis courts, swimming pool complex, poolside café, riverfront restaurant, and clubhouse.
