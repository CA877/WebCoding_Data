In Business Since 1999

Electro Painting & Refurbishing, Inc started with its first location in Chicago, IL, in 1999. We established additional locations in 2004, 2005, and 2007. At that time, EPR became the nation's largest on-site electrostatic painting company, with five offices.


Our in-house-trained crews specialize in applying high-performance coatings using the electrostatic painting process.


The coatings and application method is designed for refinishing all types of metal, including ferrous, nonferrous, anodized aluminum, stainless steel, and other conductive items. Whether at your location or ours, virtually no over spray occurs, saving our customers money by not wasting up to 50% of the costly paint used on every project. Each location has a fully equipped paint shop. 


Count on us to provide high-quality on-site electrostatic painting, shop painting, and custom painting. We utilize high-performance materials and equipment while supplying our employees with proper training and an atmosphere that rewards performance.

Our Mission

Electro Painting and Refurbishing Inc. shall provide high quality on-site electrostatic painting and shop painting services to achieve total customer satisfaction. We achieve this goal by utilizing high performance materials and equipment, while affording our employees with proper training and an atmosphere that rewards performance. This is why we are the electrostatic painting specialist that you can rely on.

On-Site Electrostatic Painting

When your project demands performance, you can rely on Electro Painting and Refurbishing to deliver a high-quality paint job on time. We specialize in the use of the Ransburg #2 electrostatic process gun which minimizes over spray but has various electrostatic equipment to meet your projects' needs.

Learn More

Interior Electrostatic Painting

Using the Ransburg #2 electrostatic spray process gun, EPR delivers high quality, near factory sprayed finish in your building with minimum of wasteful over spray, which translates into savings and less disruption for our customers. You get a great value, high quality sprayed finish on each project.

Learn More

Exterior Electrostatic Painting

EPR management teams create a custom-fitted process for surface preparation, protection, and coating system to maximize the efficiency and quality of your project. Everything metal can be painted electrostatically with high-quality performance coatings to extend the life of your metal items. Warranties up to 20 years are available!
