What Makes Davidson Day So Special?

  • Watch our video to find out

Welcome to Davidson Day School, the Best Private School in Lake Norman

Davidson Day School, located in the beautiful Lake Norman region, prepares students ages two years old through 12th grade for success in college and beyond by fostering academic excellence through collaboration, creativity, and character development. 
  • Fostering Academic Excellence
    With an 8:1 student-to-faculty ratio, Davidson Day ensures every student is well-positioned for long-term academic success.  And, our faculty are also advisors, coaches, and mentors who guide and inspire the lives of our students—and alumni.
  • Patriot Athletics
    Patriot student-athletes become the best at getting better—on the court, on the field, in the pool, on the track, in the classroom, and beyond.  That's one reason we've captured many individual and team conference and state championships.
  • Excellence in the Arts
    Our arts programs take students beyond the bounds of a page, a canvas, a screen, or the stage. Through an immersive, enriching arts program, students cultivate creativity and collaboration—and develop character.
