Characterise Nanoparticles with Unmatched Resolution

Precisely measure nanoparticle size, concentration, and zeta potential using the unmatched resolution of tunable resistive pulse sensing (TRPS) technology. The Exoid’s single-particle measurements provide clarity beyond traditional light-based methods, offering detailed analysis of lipid nanoparticles, extracellular vesicles, and more – empowering your discoveries in biology and nanomedicine.

Isolate Extracellular Vesicles for Research and Diagnostic Development

Efficiently isolate extracellular vesicles (EVs) with a scalable, gentle approach.

The qEV Isolation platform is renowned in the EV research community for its versatility and reproducibility, making it a strong choice for EV isolation.

Alongside qEV Automation solutions that minimise errors and streamline isolation workflows, qEV columns use size exclusion chromatography to deliver highly purified isolates, supporting EV research and the development of EV-based biomarkers.

Scale Nanoparticle Purification for Therapeutic or Cosmetic Development

Streamline nanoparticle purification for therapeutic and cosmetic applications with the qEV isolation platform. Combining size exclusion chromatography-based qEV columns, automation, and specialist bioprocessing support, this platform ensures high-purity isolates and process optimisation.

With tangential flow filtration systems, custom columns, and automated chromatography for cGMP-compliant environments, you can scale seamlessly from sample preparation through to purification.
