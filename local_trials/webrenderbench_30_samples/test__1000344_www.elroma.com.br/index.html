Production Process

The block is sliced by Italian knitting machines, which are controlled electronically, which optimizes production time.
The resin is for materials with microcracks, because it improves the brightness, strengthens the material and seals small cracks on the surface of the plate.
The polishing removes microcracks of the plate in order to create a smooth, opaque surface. A polishing machine does the work that generates the brightness in the material.
Storage is done in the industrial park, in a covered area and maintains the highest standard of quality.
