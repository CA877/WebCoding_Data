About Us:

In Frankfurt, Donnelly Spire was founded in 2006 by a team of three internationally experienced travel retailing and marketing executives who worked for global blue-chip companies.


It all started in Germany, a valuable market but challenging to enter successfully. The team saw an opportunity to provide a set of marketing and sales services, specifically for foreign businesses seeking to access this vital market. We de-risk the market entry by validating the market before a company incurs the costs of direct presence.


In its early years, Donnelly Spire focused on Germany and then expanded its services to cover the whole of Europe. 


Our clients are growing companies who want success the first time round in their market launch and appreciate the need for local knowledge but do not necessarily want the expense of a full direct presence on the ground immediately.
