Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Benefits of Residential Treatment for Substance Use Disorders?

During residential addiction treatment for substance use disorders and co-occurring mental health diagnoses, clients live full-time at a rehab center for the duration of their program. This ensures you receive the best treatment and support needed for healing.

Why Choose Alta Mira for Residential Substance Use Disorder Treatment?

The Alta Mira residential treatment facility in Northern California overlooks the San Francisco Bay Area, allowing us to cultivate a serene environment where you feel supported in your journey to fulfill your highest potential. With extraordinary grounds and comfortably elegant living spaces, you will experience a peaceful setting that facilitates healing and transformation.

Clients choose to receive residential help for substance use disorders at Alta Mira because they haven’t been able to achieve a stable and satisfying life despite their best efforts. This changes after a stay with us. Through our years of practice, we have observed and learned what works best for our clients, and designed a treatment dynamic that is immersive, comfortable, comprehensive, and inclusive. The result is a complete experience to support you as you cultivate a new sense of wholeness, stability, and health.

What Makes Our Alta Mira Program Unique?

Alta Mira’s treatment program for mental health, addiction, and co-occurring disorders allows each client to achieve sustainable change. In fact, many clients make more progress in a few months at Alta Mira than in years of outpatient therapy due to specialized care and amenities that create a unique residential substance use disorder treatment experience for you. Highlights include a a team of psychiatrists, masters- and doctorate-level clinicians, shared or private accommodation options, concierge treatment programming, and holistic and wellness services in our Joint Commission-accredited treatment facility.

Furthermore, our clinical team are experts in the diagnosis and treatment of mental health issues that often co-occur with drug and alcohol use disorders. By recognizing that each client is unique, with different addiction treatment goals, we’re able to employ a personalized and effective combination of psychological testing, psychiatry, psychotherapy, and integrative therapies. Using information gathered during your initial assessment, we combine evidence-based treatment modalities, group therapy, experiential, and holistic therapies to create a truly individualized residential addiction treatment program you won’t experience in any other program.

We have designed our program to make your stay at Alta Mira purposeful and life-changing. Days are structured to include three weekly one-on-one sessions to help you recover, and are paced to allow for the intensity of treatment to be balanced by time for private reflection and restorative relaxation.

How Would You Describe the Treatment Experience at Alta Mira?

At Alta Mira, we believe you’re able to do your best therapeutic work when you feel safe, welcome, and at home in your surroundings. Tucked away in the scenic neighborhood of Sausalito in Northern California, our beautiful residential treatment facility offers a choice of private and semi-private rooms to suit your needs. You can enjoy our comfortable common areas and lovely outdoor spaces with incredible views that inspire reflection and a sense of well-being.

You begin your healing process in an intimate environment with truly integrated care. Our masters- and doctorate-level team goes above and beyond to ensure your therapeutic experience is delivered with a concierge approach. Use of mobile phones, laptops, and tablets are permitted during designated hours so you have the option of keeping on top of business and personal responsibilities. Private suites are available, and we invite pets to stay with you during treatment (private room required), so you can benefit from the support they provide.

Why Would I Travel to California for Residential Treatment at Alta Mira?

Alta Mira is a national leader in residential substance use disorder treatment. Our clients come from all over the country and other parts of the world to receive help at our California rehab center. We provide a world-class, multidisciplinary treatment experience and individualized programming not found at other residential rehab centers. Getting away from the stresses of everyday living can enable you to focus on your addiction treatment.

  • Our private rehab center in Northern California provides respite, ensuring a safe, comfortable, home-like environment.
  • We have highly credentialed experts with years of experience treating a broad spectrum of substance use and co-occurring mental health disorders.
  • All of our clients receive comprehensive psychological and neuropsychological testing to make sure we get an accurate diagnosis.
  • We collaborate with all your current healthcare providers to provide the best possible care.
  • Many clients make more progress in a residential alcohol and drug addiction treatment stay at Alta Mira than they have in years of outpatient therapy.
