UNIREP

Unirep is a privately owned Trading, Agency, Distribution and Logistics company based in Istanbul, Turkey. The company’s mission is :

To be the most “sought after” supplier of Production Materials and Investment Equipment To the Paper and Hygiene related Industries In Turkey and the surrounding Markets.

In harmony with this mission, the Company aspires to be the leading supplier of products and services to the Paper and Hygiene related Industries in her Territory. This endeavor is enhanced with Unirep’s vision; to avail the utmost added-value in services and material quality to her customers, expose and sustain innovative market potentials to her suppliers, fulfill mankind’s universally accepted values and reach a business excellence in order to promote further investments from her shareholders.
